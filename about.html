About Us

Jubo Shokti Debidwar was formed by young people in Debidwar who wanted to take an active role in their community. We believe change starts with organized, honest effort at the local level.

Our Vision

Suppose every young person in Debidwar had a way to contribute their time and skills to something bigger than themselves — that's the future we're working toward.

Our Mission

To organize, train, and mobilize the youth of Debidwar around community service, civic awareness, and local development projects.
